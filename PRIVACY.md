# Calendar Hours privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Calendar Hours reads the title, start time, end time, and date fields of a Google Calendar event on the open Calendar page. It uses those fields locally in your browser to calculate an elapsed duration and propose or insert hours or minutes in the event title. When Dynamic is enabled for a matching phrase or the existing-duration update setting is on, it detects when you move or resize a qualifying one-time event on the Calendar grid, displays the new duration immediately, then opens that event in an inactive Calendar tab, corrects its title, and uses Google Calendar's own Save button. The existing-duration update setting starts on for new installations and can be turned off in Settings. Calendar then saves the event under your Google account as it normally does. Calendar Hours does not access other sites or use the Google Calendar API.

Calendar Hours does not collect, sell, or share calendar event data with its developer or other services. It has no analytics, advertising, account system, server, or network requests of its own. When optional automatic saving is enabled, the extension's click on Calendar's Save button causes Calendar to send the edited event to Google as part of the normal save flow. Your formatting and trigger preferences are stored using the browser's extension storage. If browser sync is enabled, the browser provider may sync those preferences under your browser account. No event titles or event times are stored there.

The extension requests access to `calendar.google.com/calendar/*` because that is where the event editor appears. It requests the `storage` permission solely for preferences. You can remove the extension and its stored preferences in your browser's Extensions settings.

For questions about this policy, use the support contact listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
