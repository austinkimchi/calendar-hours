Why Calendar access?

Connecting Google Calendar lets the extension read and edit events you can access and read your calendar list. It uses those permissions to correct qualifying event titles after time changes and to show calendars available for an optional Workday import.

The extension processes calendar data in your browser and sends title saves to Google Calendar. A Workday import sends selected event times, and descriptions only if you enable that option, to your Workday organization. The developer does not receive your events.

Control stays with you

  • Connect in the extension’s settings.
  • Enable fast saves or Workday import separately.
  • Disconnect Google Calendar in settings at any time.
  • Read the full privacy policy.
