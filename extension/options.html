Workday import Preview

In Workday, choose Actions → Auto-fill from Calendar.

  1. Connect Google Calendar in General, then choose your work calendar here.
  2. Enter a phrase to match event titles and choose the time zone shown in Workday.
  3. Save, then open Workday's Enter Time page. Auto-fill from Calendar imports into the day or week you are viewing.
This feature is a developer preview.
