Calendar Hours

Choose when durations appear and how they look.

General

Add a phrase for each kind of event you want Calendar Hours to recognize.

Matching phraseDynamic
Dynamic updates the duration when you change event times or move or resize a matching one-time event. After a grid change, it saves the corrected title through Google Calendar. Repeating events need manual review.

Matching ignores case and uses whole words or phrases.

Hours

When enabled, longer events still use hours. Choosing Minutes above uses minutes for every duration.

Hours format

Use {value} for the number and {unit} for the unit.

Minutes format

When you first enable minutes, this starts with your Hours title template. You can then customize it separately.

3 hours 10 minutes: Work session (3.17 hours)
45 minutes: Work session (0.75 hours)
1 hour: Work session (1 hour)
