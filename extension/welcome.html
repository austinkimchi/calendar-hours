Keep event durations up to date

Calendar Hours adds the length of a Google Calendar event to its title and can import your work blocks into Workday. Connect Google Calendar once, then choose the features you want in settings.

What connecting allows

One Google authorization request covers viewing and editing events and reading your calendar list. The connection supports faster title saves and Workday calendar import. Features have separate on/off settings. Event data and your access token are not sent to a Calendar Hours server.
