Keep event durations up to date

Calendar Hours adds the length of a Google Calendar event to its title. Connect your Google account for faster title updates when you move or resize an event.

What connecting allows

Google asks for permission to view and edit events on your calendars. When you move or resize a qualifying event, Calendar Hours reads that event, waits for its new time to save, and updates its title. Event data and your access token are not sent to a Calendar Hours server.
